MSF XR Accessibility Guidelines

The Accessibility in the Metaverse Working Group is building a website for the Metaverse Standards Forum (MSF) XR Accessibility Guidelines: testable accessibility criteria and real-world examples for virtual, augmented, and mixed reality. Together, these are called extended reality (XR).

The guidelines are not on this site yet. Version 1.0 was released for public review in July 2026, and we are now working on version 2.0. Until the site is ready, please read the working document.

  • Working document

    Read the current draft of the guidelines and comment on it. Opens in Google Docs.

  • Feedback form

    Tell us what works, what is missing, and what to change. The form was written for version 1.0, and we still want to hear from you. Opens in Google Forms.

  • Working group page

    Learn about the working group, its meetings, and how to join.

Try our drafts

For discussion only. The working group has not approved these drafts. They may change or be removed. They are not part of the guidelines, and they are not evidence that any product conforms.

What we are planning

These plans for version 2.0 come from the working document and may change:

  • a new platform where you can filter by tags, with links between criteria and examples;
  • refined criteria, and more examples;
  • further suggestions and tools for auditing XR applications and meeting the criteria.

Other ways to take part

  • Email the chairs at accessibility-chair@lists.metaverse-standards.org. Please tell us if you find an accessibility barrier on this site.
  • See the source files in our GitHub repository. Pull requests from people who are not MSF members are reviewed by the Forum membership before they are accepted.
